# Facilitator Guide — Breakout Sessions & Idea Board

This guide is for you (the trainer/facilitator). Attendees do not use the Idea Board app — you capture their ideas on their behalf during report-backs.

## 1. Setting up breakout groups

- Aim for 5–7 people per group, ~6–9 groups for 45 attendees.
- Mix roles/departments per group where possible — cross-functional groups surface more varied ideas and avoid groupthink.
- Assign or ask each group to nominate a spokesperson for the report-back.

## 2. Running the breakout discussion (60 min)

Give each group a simple prompt, anchored to the business goals captured at the start of the day:

> "Thinking about [business goal(s) captured this morning], where in your day-to-day work could Copilot save you time, reduce errors, or help you serve clients better? Come up with as many ideas as you can, then pick your top 2–3."

Encourage groups to think across all 5 topic areas covered in the art-of-the-possible session, not just one. Use the Idea Capture Worksheet (`idea-capture-worksheet.md`) as an optional paper scaffold — groups can write directly on it or just talk it through and report back verbally.

Circulate between groups to:
- Unblock groups that are stuck (offer 1–2 estate-agency-relevant examples per topic, e.g. Copilot drafting property listing descriptions, summarising viewing feedback emails, or a Copilot Studio agent answering tenant maintenance queries).
- Note down particularly strong ideas so you don't miss them later.

## 3. Capturing ideas in the Idea Board app (during report-backs)

Open `app/index.html` in a browser (no install or internet needed). For each idea a group reports:

1. Fill in **Group/Team name**, **Topic**, **Idea title**, **Business goal it supports**, **Description**, and **Impact/effort notes**.
2. Set **Status**:
   - **Idea only** — good idea, not being demoed today, keep for the backlog.
   - **Shortlisted** — the idea is strong enough to build a quick demo/test scenario before the final showcase. Fill in the **Demo scenario** and **Presenter(s)** fields.
3. Click **Add idea**. It appears instantly in the matching column (Idea only / Shortlisted).

You can re-tag at any point: use **⭐ Shortlist** or **↩ Move back** on any card, or **Delete** to remove a duplicate/mistake.

Use the **filters** (topic/status dropdowns) to review ideas by topic when deciding what to shortlist.

## 4. Confirming the shortlist

After all groups have reported back, review the **Shortlisted** column together as a room (or with group spokespeople) and confirm:
- Is the number of shortlisted ideas realistic for the remaining build time? (Rule of thumb: 1 demo per 45–60 min of build time available.)
- Does each shortlisted idea have a clear owner/presenter?

Move any idea that doesn't make the cut back to **Idea only** rather than deleting it — it's still useful input for follow-up conversations.

## 5. During demo/build time

Support groups building their shortlisted idea (e.g. a Copilot Studio agent, an M365 Copilot prompt, a GitHub Copilot-assisted script or report). Keep the Idea Board open to reference each group's description/demo plan as a reminder of what they committed to.

## 6. Final showcase

Before the showcase, click **Print Showcase Cards** — this generates one printable page per shortlisted idea (title, business goal, description, demo plan, presenter) that you can hand out or display as a prompt/running order.

## 7. Wrap-up

At the end of the day:
- Click **Export JSON** and **Export CSV** to save the full idea board (both idea-only and shortlisted) as a leave-behind artifact for David James leadership.
- If you ran breakouts across multiple rooms/devices, use **Import JSON** on one device to merge all idea sets before exporting the final combined file (duplicates by ID are skipped automatically).
